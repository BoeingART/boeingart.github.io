## Research Experience

**Data-Driven Discovery & Active Learning for Cooperative Catalysis**  
Co-first author / project leader (NSFC Ph.D. project), Nature Synth. (2026), Mar. 2024 - Present

- Developed Bayesian optimization algorithms tailored for small-sample, high-noise organic synthesis, addressing search challenges in high-dimensional chemical spaces.
- Implemented an automated DFT descriptor extraction pipeline integrated with Bayesian optimization, enabling a one-stop "reaction design-feature extraction-active learning optimization" platform.
- Achieved global optimization of a 1.8-million reaction space within only 63 experiments, significantly accelerating reaction discovery.

**Ullmann Reaction Prediction & SynAD Reliability Framework**  
First author / project leader (NSFC undergraduate project), Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. (2026), Sept. 2023 - Oct. 2025

- Built a high-accuracy Ullmann reaction yield prediction model by integrating physical organic chemistry knowledge with automated DFT-based descriptor computation.
- Proposed the Synthetic Applicability Domain (SynAD) framework to address the extrapolation trap of AI in chemical synthesis and establish uncertainty assessment in unknown chemical spaces.
- Validated the framework on seven in-house and public datasets, showing that SynAD effectively filters unreliable predictions and improves model reliability.

**Computer Vision for Polymer Miscibility Prediction**  
Second author, J. Chem. Inf. Model. (2023), May 2022 - July 2023

- Constructed a deep convolutional neural network (CNN) visual model to automatically extract high-level features from SEM microscopic images of polymer blends.
- Enabled rapid automatic characterization of miscibility and related material properties, replacing inefficient manual analysis workflows.
